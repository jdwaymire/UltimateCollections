Home Screen

Shows collections as rows on your users' home screens (Emby 4.10 or newer). Seasonal rows appear when their season starts and hide when it ends, in the spot each user gave them. Rows change when the sync runs.

Off. Using another plugin for home screens (for example Home Screen Companion)? Let only one of them manage collection rows, or users may see a row twice.
